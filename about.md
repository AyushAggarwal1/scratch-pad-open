# Hi, I'm Ayush.

I'm a TPM working on cloud-security products. My work sits where product questions meet technical decisions: understanding the problem, defining what to build, and making the details clear enough to act on.
{: .page-lede }

This notebook is a window into that process. It brings together product requirements, architecture notes, research, and practical commands from my work across cloud identity, data security, and application security.

## What you'll find in my work

<div class="about-principles"><div><span>01</span><h3>Start with the problem.</h3><p>Define the user, the decision they need to make, and the constraints. Turn a broad product idea into a focused first version.</p></div><div><span>02</span><h3>Stay close to the systems.</h3><p>Work through permissions, data flows, APIs, and failure cases. Connect the product experience to the mechanics underneath.</p></div><div><span>03</span><h3>Make the thinking visible.</h3><p>Write down the requirements, open questions, and tradeoffs. Keep the references and small tools that make the next step easier.</p></div></div>
